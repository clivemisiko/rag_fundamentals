# QuantumCorp Technical Protocols & Operating Manual (v4.2 — 2026)

## 1. Phase-7 Flux Reactor Operational Specifications
The Phase-7 Flux Reactor powers the primary quantum propulsion manifold for Project Aetheria. Under normal cruising conditions, the core chamber operates between 3,200 kPa and 4,400 kPa of pressure. The maximum permissible operating pressure is strictly 4,850 kPa. If the internal pressure exceeds 4,850 kPa, the automated Argon-18 emergency purge sequence is triggered within 450 milliseconds to prevent core rupture. The reactor uses CryoHydrate-9 as its primary coolant (density: 1.42 g/cm³), which requires full recirculation and replenishment every 72 operational cycles. Any coolant leakage greater than 50 mL per hour requires an immediate Level-1 shutdown.
